# Research comparable niche competitors

Read [PROJECT_CONTRACT](../PROJECT_CONTRACT.md) before using this skill in the network. Role: **phase1-support**; it contributes the scoped task below and does not silently expand a domain build. Use current site facts, authorization and actual tool capabilities.

## Workflow

1. Read the active niche brief, then inspect a compact local/international sample using available web/browser tools. Record URLs, market, retrieval date and whether evidence is visual or text-only.
2. Separate observable offerings, layouts and prices from design judgements. Competitor capacity and proof are never our business facts.
3. Map reader problem to useful pattern, our adaptation, dependency and include/defer/reject. Feed the builder and planner instead of stopping a complete site task at the report.

## Handoff

A dated comparison matrix, actual evidence, adopted patterns and unresolved access limits.
