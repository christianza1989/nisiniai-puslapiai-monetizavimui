What this website preview stores, and what it does not.

Website demonstration

The homepage uses fictional sample apps and data. Messages, email drafts, notes and spreadsheet edits stay in page memory and clear on reset or reload. The video player uses local preview files; it does not sign in to YouTube or your other accounts.

Saved setup

The shop saves only your chosen package and holder finish in this browser’s local storage when you choose Save my setup. You can replace it by saving another setup or remove it by clearing this site’s browser data.

Contacting us

The contact form prepares a draft in your email application. This website does not store or send that message. Sending it through your email app shares its contents with hello@phonebridger.com and your email provider.

Account preview

On the local account preview, the server stores your email, creation time, a salted password hash and session records. It does not store a readable password. A first-party HttpOnly session cookie keeps the preview signed in and expires after eight hours. Logging out clears that session.

These are local preview accounts, not production licences or purchases. Hosted account operations, email verification and automatic password recovery are not active. Do not use a password you use elsewhere in this preview.

Current website scope

No live checkout, marketing analytics or advertising tracker is added by these pages. The downloaded apps request their own permissions during setup; this note describes the website preview, not a complete production app privacy policy.

Before public launch

Production hosting, legal operator details, retention/contact rights and account policies require review before live accounts or orders are opened.
