# Editorial quality review

Before returning output, challenge it as a reader and as an evidence reviewer. Correct problems inside the current operation; remaining blockers must name the precise claim/dependency and next action. Do not merely append "verify everything" or demand owner review of ordinary editorial choices.

## Plan checks

Apply [planning decisions and handover](planning-decisions.md): the first inspectable artifact maps head queries and existing URLs; important decisions show what research changed; full-map acceptance is separate from draft/release acceptance. For a single-page draft reuse its reconciled brief rather than repeating full-map research.

For an initial new-niche plan, verify the business decision rests on inspected buying-intent/competitor evidence, alternative revenue models, fulfilment feasibility, sourced or explicitly assumed economics and a meaningful test. A generic “later affiliate/ads/leads” sentence is insufficient. For an ordinary article reuse the current business decision and check only relevant changed facts; do not force a whole new business study.

**Business fit first:** is the selected customer, paid outcome and plausible revenue mechanism explicit in the brief, and does the initial offer collect relevant product/service intent? An old calendar or attractive informational topic is insufficient. If ordinary strategy remains open, research and choose it; if a real operational fact is unresolved, keep that claim private. A topic suggestion is editorial interest, not a purchase enquiry. Assess helpful informational pages by their role in the customer journey without forcing a sales pitch into every paragraph.

1. **Specificity:** could a target reader explain what decision each URL helps them make? Does each add an actual checklist, explanation, example or workable procedure? Would changing only the domain make the plan fit any niche? If yes, rewrite.
2. **Distinctness:** compare new pages with each other and existing inventory by question/answer, not slug. Consolidate overlapping intents before generation. Similar words alone do not prove cannibalization; use actual query/page data for performance diagnoses.
3. **Architecture:** compare every support question against its root title/scope: could the root reasonably summarize and introduce this answer? No Christmas parent for all holidays, winter-storage parent for general inspection, missing roots, self-reference or cycles. Choose a broad useful root and make narrow subtopics its children. Roots precede supports in returned order and planned timing. Each important page has a plausible discovery path.
4. **Timing:** every date is real, in bounds and in the right local year; relevant preparation topics precede events. Movable dates require evidence. Evergreen hooks must be exactly empty, even when you scheduled the page first or before winter. Put dependency/cadence rationale in reason instead. Lead times remain assumptions; no ranking/indexing deadline claims.
5. **Honest delivery:** title, description and reason agree. No unbuilt downloads/tools, invented operational capacity, other sites' contacts, unsupported product stock or fake expertise.

## Draft checks

1. **Answer:** the opening answers the specific question with its limits. A reader can act on the explanation without rereading generic paragraphs. The body fulfils the title.
2. **Contribution:** practical steps, criteria or a clearly labelled worked example add value beyond restating sources. No filler word quotas or repeated keyword text.
3. **Evidence:** inspect important numbers, units, dates, safety, model compatibility, prices, legal/medical/financial claims and business promises. Each has applicable current evidence or is omitted with a precise private note. Generated examples are identified as examples.
4. **Sources:** retrieve the document, check the supported passage and context; a plausible URL, snippet, candidate or HTTP 200 is insufficient. Prefer original/official evidence. Distinguish source guidance from our own assumptions and offer.
5. **Links:** anchors describe genuine targets; reasons are useful to the reader; no invented IDs, wrong domain, self-links, unverified citations or future-route leakage. Check reciprocal discovery when publication occurs, not by exposing drafts.
6. **Assets/authorship:** every initial guide/blog has its inspected topic-specific image, and homepage/index supporting imagery is complete. Actual optimized files render at desktop/mobile with correct focal point, alt, dimensions and same-source variants. Text-focused privacy/legal exceptions have a reason. No generator badges on original imagery; retain private/editorial provenance and required third-party credit. Any promised file/function is present and works. No fabricated authors, credentials, reviews, hands-on testing or client work.
7. **Language:** natural local language; no editorial labels in headings; no unsupported superlatives, fixed promises or boilerplate CTA unrelated to actual functionality.

## Evidence handoff

Where storage permits, record claim, exact source URL, retrievedAt, applicable model/jurisdiction/year, supported passage in your own words, reviewer and outcome: confirmed/revise/remove/unresolved. Respect source reuse limits. The current studio JSON schemas have no complete evidence-ledger field; use `sourceQueries`, `reason`, `externalSources.reason` and precise `factChecks` without adding schema keys or pretending a ledger exists.

Publication checks belong to orchestration: functioning contacts, real asset files, domain isolation, approved immutable revision and time gate, indexable public URL, matching canonical/sitemap/robots/schema, durable inquiry delivery, privacy and measured mobile performance. Skill adherence alone is not evidence those checks passed.
