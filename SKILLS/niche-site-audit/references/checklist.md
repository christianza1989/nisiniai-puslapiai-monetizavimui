# A–Z acceptance checklist

One maintained criterion catalog. Format: ID | stage | gate/review | criterion. `local` is current prepared-site quality; `launch` needs actual production evidence; `operations` follows launch. Every applicable item contributes to its stage's score. `gate` failures/unverified evidence block readiness. `NA` requires a concrete scope explanation, never missing access. Mark checks in each site's report, not in this reusable catalog.

## A — Phase and offer
- [ ] A1 | local | gate | Phase 1 is explicit; inquiry is not an order, reservation or proven demand.
- [ ] A2 | local | gate | The primary action and visible offer are genuinely available and test the chosen concrete business outcome with a documented payer/revenue hypothesis; editorial interest does not substitute for product/service intent. No fake commerce, stock or supplier claim; prelaunch availability is disclosed.
- [ ] A3 | operations | gate | Expansion decision uses qualified real inquiries/value/capacity, not clicks or test records.
## B — Business truth and contacts
- [ ] B1 | local | gate | Operator/contact defaults and site exceptions agree across package, visible copy, schema and form recipient.
- [ ] B2 | local | gate | No borrowed phones, addresses, identities, credentials, reviews or certifications.
- [ ] B3 | launch | gate | Actual operator identity, applicable legal identifiers/address and domain control are established.
## C — Domain history
- [ ] C1 | local | gate | Bounded history evidence, retrieval limits and unavailable periods are recorded.
- [ ] C2 | local | gate | Reviewed URL decisions distinguish same-intent restore/redirect from defer/404; no mass homepage redirects.
- [ ] C3 | launch | review | Any implemented legacy redirect has a current same-host approved 200 target, no loop and a tested status.
## D — Research and original value
- [ ] D1 | local | review | Local/international comparisons and real desktop/mobile evidence support the chosen journey; adopted design decisions identify the actual final URL and useful screenshot region, with blocked/blank/overlaid evidence limitations recorded.
- [ ] D2 | local | gate | Current facts and permissions support original copy/assets; old or competitor claims are not our facts.
- [ ] D3 | local | review | Search intents and niche advantages are hypotheses where no search/conversion data exists.
## E — Visual identity
- [ ] E1 | local | review | Full homepage, index and guide have a coherent niche-specific identity, rhythm and meaningful imagery; for a new identity, actual nearest-network comparison demonstrates substantive differences beyond noun, palette, font or photo-subject substitutions.
- [ ] E2 | local | review | DESIGN describes actual tokens, composition, brand/section/asset plan, useful-tool decision and compromises; technical scores are separate from visual judgment.
- [ ] E3 | local | gate | Image origin/rights are documented and presentation is truthful; no imaginary stock, client project, distorted teaching diagram or unwanted generator badge.
## F — Information architecture
- [ ] F1 | local | gate | All public pages are reachable through useful navigation/context; no orphan initial guide.
- [ ] F2 | local | gate | Desktop/mobile header, index, footer and local inquiry actions work with actual destinations.
- [ ] F3 | local | review | Each URL has a distinct job; no doorway city/synonym variants, duplicate intent or pointless index.
## G — Homepage and action
- [ ] G1 | local | gate | First screen identifies the topic, useful offer and honest next action.
- [ ] G2 | local | review | Middle/end answer new questions rather than repeating promotions or decorative cards.
- [ ] G3 | local | gate | Primary/secondary actions, privacy route, empty/error/success states have real behavior.
## H — Initial guides and useful content
- [ ] H1 | local | gate | At least three distinct prepared guides are individually read and useful for the site's intent; no word-count substitute.
- [ ] H2 | local | gate | Claims, terminology, examples, limitations and sources are checked; unsafe universal technical advice is absent.
- [ ] H3 | local | review | Each guide gives a usable explanation/example/checklist, readable structure and next step without filler.
- [ ] H4 | local | gate | Long article, lists, figures and source sections are actually rendered and mobile-tested, not silently discarded.
- [ ] H5 | local | gate | Each initial guide has an inspected topic-specific image; homepage/index and other pages have purposeful visual coverage, actual files and responsive crops, or a documented text-focused reason where imagery adds no value.
## I — Authorship and dates
- [ ] I1 | local | gate | Visible attribution identifies a real Person or Organization with a public profile or clear identity.
- [ ] I2 | local | gate | Author/profile/schema identity agrees; no fictional expert or unverified experience.
- [ ] I3 | local | gate | Visible publication/review dates and structured dates have the same meaning; dates are not refreshed per request.
- [ ] I4 | launch | gate | Initial production publication/deployment date is documented; planned dates are not evidence of past public availability.
## J — Editorial trust
- [ ] J1 | local | gate | About/editorial information explains purpose, AI role, source method, limitations and corrections contact.
- [ ] J2 | local | review | Review is attributed honestly to the agent/process; no claim of human/qualified approval without it.
- [ ] J3 | local | gate | Factual corrections propagate through studio approval and public/LLM projections; review evidence is retained.
## K — On-page metadata
- [ ] K1 | local | gate | Every public URL has one useful H1, title, description, correct language and canonical.
- [ ] K2 | local | review | Heading order, informative alt, sharing metadata and social image (when used) match actual content; new-site wordmark/mark and actual favicon belong to the active niche rather than an inherited unrelated brand.
- [ ] K3 | local | gate | Unknown/future/private URLs return proper 404/noindex and do not canonicalize to the homepage.
## L — Structured data
- [ ] L1 | local | gate | JSON-LD parses and uses truthful appropriate WebSite/WebPage/Organization/Article entities and stable IDs.
- [ ] L2 | local | gate | Visible breadcrumb and schema path/name/URL agree; profile and author relations point to eligible public pages.
- [ ] L3 | local | gate | No invented Offer/Product/Review/AggregateRating/LocalBusiness or unsupported rich-result promise.
- [ ] L4 | launch | review | Official rich-result/URL Inspection findings are recorded after actual crawlable deployment; local checks are labelled local.
## M — Internal linking
- [ ] M1 | local | gate | Contextual links use real same-site target IDs and informative anchors; fragments exist.
- [ ] M2 | local | gate | Future/unapproved/revoked targets disappear consistently from prose, related sections, indexes and schema.
- [ ] M3 | local | review | Cluster/pillar/related routes help distinct questions; repetition/all-to-all links are not treated as authority.
## N — External/network linking
- [ ] N1 | local | gate | Sources have relevant primary evidence, actual target checks and retrieval dates; no partner implication.
- [ ] N2 | local | gate | Owned editorial links obey target ID, host, approval/date/deployment eligibility and disclosed relevant reason.
- [ ] N3 | local | gate | Prose links and source/related panels render correctly; sponsored/UGC relation is applied only if applicable.
- [ ] N4 | launch | review | Brand/attribution and other external destinations work on the live launch; pending network domains stay unpublished.
## O — Crawl and URL behavior
- [ ] O1 | local | gate | Canonical host/path, sitemap, robots, redirects and slash/query behavior are coherent and actually tested.
- [ ] O2 | local | gate | Sitemap contains only eligible URLs and meaningful lastmod; robots does not substitute for private access control.
- [ ] O3 | launch | gate | DNS, TLS, real host, indexing directives and domain isolation work on production.
## P — Publication and isolation
- [ ] P1 | local | gate | Same authoritative projection controls HTML, links, media, schema, sitemap and LLM output.
- [ ] P2 | local | gate | Hash/date/revocation/cross-host negative tests pass; draft preview is private and not indexed.
- [ ] P3 | local | gate | Studio approval, import validation and compile preserve unrelated packages and reject changed approved content.
## Q — GEO and machine-readable content
- [ ] Q1 | local | gate | Public LLM exports match the active niche's useful facts, URLs, contacts and visible publication scope.
- [ ] Q2 | local | gate | Definitions, qualified answers and primary citations are accessible in semantic HTML; no hidden model-only claims.
- [ ] Q3 | local | review | llms.txt/AI visibility are supplementary; no special schema, traffic or ranking guarantee is asserted.
## R — Accessibility
- [ ] R1 | local | gate | Keyboard, focus, skip link, labels, landmarks and details/menu interactions are exercised.
- [ ] R2 | local | gate | Contrast, zoom/reflow, readable utility text and touch targets are checked; score alone is not WCAG conformance.
- [ ] R3 | local | gate | Images, headings, disclosure state, form requirements/errors and reduced-motion behavior remain usable.
## S — Responsive reading
- [ ] S1 | local | gate | Desktop, narrow/mobile and tablet evidence shows no overflow or hidden defects.
- [ ] S2 | local | gate | Article contents, byline, source lists, breadcrumb, form and footer work at narrow widths and enlarged text.
- [ ] S3 | local | review | Real viewport tests are distinguished from physical device and synthesized touch testing.
## T — Performance
- [ ] T1 | local | gate | Production mobile lab is measured with real media and saved version/date/environment; performance target >=90 is met or remains failed.
- [ ] T2 | local | review | Measured LCP/CLS/TBT, image sizes, fonts, CSS and JS budgets have justified fixes; no dummy content score.
- [ ] T3 | launch | review | Production field CWV/traffic evidence is monitored separately; local Lighthouse is not field performance.
## U — Inquiry capture
- [ ] U1 | local | gate | Native/server validation, origin, size limits and honest errors pass; durable D1 record survives mail/core failure.
- [ ] U2 | local | gate | Operator notification recipient and SMTP acceptance are tested without client messages or exposed secrets.
- [ ] U3 | local | gate | Matching marked Message-ID INBOX evidence is distinct from SMTP authentication/acceptance; tests do not inflate demand.
- [ ] U4 | launch | gate | Actual production D1/bindings, delivery, recovery/reconciliation and spam/rate controls are verified.
## V — Measurement and decisions
- [ ] V1 | local | gate | Per-site counters exclude bots/DNT/GPC/tests as supported; clicks are separate from received inquiries.
- [ ] V2 | local | gate | Measurement privacy statements match stored fields/cookies/identifiers and enabled providers.
- [ ] V3 | operations | gate | GSC/analytics/qualified inquiries and testing interval support the niche decision; voice is off unless separately authorized and gated.
## W — Privacy and terms
- [ ] W1 | local | gate | Notice and usage terms match an information/inquiry pilot, actual data inventory and current authoritative requirements.
- [ ] W2 | local | gate | Purpose, contact, rights, processors/transfer scope and storage/deletion limits are stated truthfully; no invented policy or legal identity.
- [ ] W3 | launch | gate | Production legal basis, recipients/processors, transfers, retention and delete/recovery process are established and accurate in the public notice.
- [ ] W4 | local | gate | Nonessential cookie/marketing consent is implemented only when actually needed; inquiry is not blanket marketing consent.
## X — Security and operations
- [ ] X1 | local | gate | No secrets/private leads/drafts in Git, public bundle, media paths, logs or LLM output; tenant and payload boundaries tested.
- [ ] X2 | local | review | Error behavior, dependence on optional voice/core, headers, spam vectors and recovery gaps are assessed with evidence.
- [ ] X3 | launch | gate | Production access, abuse limits, backups/restore and incident controls are working; unresolved risk is not hidden by a score.
## Y — Reuse and regression
- [ ] Y1 | local | gate | Shared fixes live in core; site-specific identity/content remain isolated; schema change updates both validators and integration tests.
- [ ] Y2 | local | gate | Core/SEO regressions pass all current niches after relevant changes, with actual commands/results.
- [ ] Y3 | local | gate | START_HERE, AGENTS, builder/planner and site journal link the current acceptance workflow for a fresh session.
## Z — Evidence and handover
- [ ] Z1 | local | gate | Checklist, baseline findings, repairs, screenshots, versions, remaining blockers and score evidence are stored per site.
- [ ] Z2 | local | gate | 10/10/local-ready/domain-ready claims obey the score contract; failed/unverified checks are visible.
- [ ] Z3 | launch | gate | Final domain-ready handover has all applicable launch gates proved; no unmeasured guarantee of demand or ranking.
