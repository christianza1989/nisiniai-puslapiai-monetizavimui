# International pattern research

## Choose comparable examples

Translate the customer job and industry terminology, not just the domain name. Search local and foreign queries for products/services, selection questions and specific use cases. For tractor tyres this may include agricultural tyres/tractor tyres, pneus agricoles, Traktorreifen/Landwirtschaftsreifen and opony rolnicze. This is an example, not a fixed market list for every niche.

Market fit matters: B2B vs consumer, distribution vs direct sales, local service vs ecommerce, pricing tax convention, delivery territory, language, seasonal conditions and the customer's expertise. A US fitment flow may provide a useful pattern without supplying valid Lithuanian specifications, pricing or service promises.

## Inspect the actual journey

| Surface | What to observe | What to record/adapt |
|---|---|---|
| Header/desktop nav | Brand recognition, category hierarchy, search, contact and primary action | Short useful paths, not every competitor's menu entry |
| Mobile header | Menu discoverability, readable tap targets, sticky controls, obstruction | A compact genuine navigation that fits the content; sticky UI only if useful |
| Hero | Customer question, promise, relevant imagery, next action | Clear truthful offer and useful starting point; no borrowed operational claims |
| Categories/selection | Dimension, use case, model, service filters; empty/result states | Real data/selection when available; in phase one a guide or request-preparation path |
| Detail/service page | Specifications, limits, price/tax, stock, suitability, delivery, evidence | Verified information and limits; fake catalogue cards are rejected |
| Guide/info hub | Clear answer, examples, sources, structure, link to next decision | Distinct useful questions, coherent clusters, original explanation |
| Proof | Actual staff, projects, evidence, reviews | Replace unsupported proof with demonstrably useful guidance and sources |
| Contact/form | Required information, errors, privacy, confirmation, contact channels | A small working inquiry form with truthful outcomes; no invented phone or SLA |
| Footer | Contact identity, useful links, policies, support routes | Genuine pages and attribution; no copied certifications/payment badges |
| Overall visual system | Density, hierarchy, palette, typography, imagery, space, rhythm | Justified original design direction, not a screenshot clone |

Inspect at least a representative homepage and key detail/guide on desktop and mobile when visual access is available. Capture only enough evidence to support decisions. Do not change another business's cart, account, form or preferences to research it; read-only inspection is sufficient. Keep screenshots local as research evidence, not published site assets.

## Evidence row format

`URL | country/market | checkedAt | pageType | evidence (rendered screenshot/DOM/retrieved text) | observed pattern | customer benefit hypothesis | adaptation | prerequisite | now/defer/reject`

Add artifact paths for screenshots and note access failures. Retrieval alone does not prove visual appearance. A static button alone does not prove backend behavior. Read actual public information for a competitor price or delivery claim and preserve its context/date; do not make cross-model price comparisons look equivalent.

Record the final observed URL and the screenshot region supporting each adopted design decision. A homepage screenshot is not evidence of an article URL's composition. Blank lazy sections or cookie overlays cannot establish a full-page design. Separate competitor capabilities from visual references and apply [design diversity](design-diversity.md) when translating the findings into this network's identity and asset plan.

## Decide and stop researching

Synthesize the strongest patterns when additional sites repeat what is already known. Prefer a few coherent choices over a collage of every attractive section. Resolve these questions before code:

1. What is the visitor trying to decide in the first screen?
2. What factual uncertainty can our phase-one site reduce?
3. Which pattern makes that decision easier and what evidence shows it exists?
4. Can we implement its actual capability now? If not, what honest alternative serves the same job?
5. What does our design deliberately omit because we lack proof or phase-two operations?

Do not present a universal numeric design score derived from the matrix. Compare usability criteria and measured performance separately.
