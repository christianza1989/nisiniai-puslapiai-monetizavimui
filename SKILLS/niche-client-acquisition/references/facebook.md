Separately verify platform authorization, group rules, data use, current offer and the tool's actual consequential-action requirements. A research/build task in another context does not automatically inherit messaging permission. No owner prompt or group rule grants Meta automation rights.

## Inspect and qualify

Use the approved browser capability for authorized UI review; a library/tool name is not a license. Do not enable a continuous personal-profile collector while its platform permission is UNVERIFIED. Do not export group member lists, private conversations or forbidden post content. Preserve only minimal permitted facts; when reuse is restricted return anonymous channel findings rather than a prospect database.

For each group record the observed URL/ID, topic/language/visibility, rules and inspection date, allowed actions/data use, membership evidence and relevant sites. Search-result popularity is not buyer demand. Inspect original timing and classify buyer, provider, referral, recruitment and procurement separately. A group city does not establish a post's location. Unknown recency remains unknown.

Read current BUSINESS and contact config. Auksarankiams' initial furniture/adjustment/hanging scope excludes electrical, gas, plumbing and structural work; apply the actual site's exclusions rather than a generic handyman offer. Padangų advice does not certify fit, supply or stock. A provider posting in a group is not automatically a website buyer.

## Draft and execute only through a valid channel

Offer a concise useful answer or one relevant clarifying question; disclose the actual relationship to our project. Do not impersonate an independent customer, invent experience/partners/prices or promise fulfilment. A live relevant link may help; do not distribute unrelated network ads. A prelaunch enquiry stays transparent.

A request for PM is scoped to that request. Do not equate a personal DM, group author, Page comment and official Page Messenger recipient. Do not import the owner's whole personal inbox. Official Page messaging needs the actual account/app permissions and channel-policy checks from the plan; evaluate window eligibility server-side from real events again before delayed sending.

Return the exact site/account/group/thread/target, factual offer version, draft, mode, unmet gates and next action. The coordinator checks the current target and draft revision immediately before a write and records the actual receipt. Pending moderation, a timeout and visible publication are different outcomes. An uncertain submit is not permission to retry blindly.

## Case handoff and measurement

For original Page content use a content-job/calendar purpose with factual/asset review, publishAt, actor, eventual provider ID/permalink and publication receipt. Do not manufacture a buyer signal for a post. Keep the social calendar separate from website Article releases, while reusing shared factual/media conventions. Use original useful demonstrations/questions rather than fake engagement or copied broadcasts. Real critical feedback is not hidden merely to improve sales appearance; moderation has its own rules/action scope.

Diagnose reach/reactions/comments, but optimise for qualified use, attributable sales, repeat use and contribution after acquisition/media/AI/support/fulfilment/refund costs. Keep group-intent and Page-content cohorts separate. Unknown insights/costs/attribution stay unknown; likes and small samples do not prove profit. Weekly learning changes one main hypothesis with baseline/rollback; it cannot change approved prices or permissions. Incoming discussion replies need separate capacity from proactive pitches.

A found public request remains a signal. Only actual project-directed inbound can become a received case source; preserve original channel IDs and reuse the existing core registry. Page-scoped identity is not a global person key; no cross-site case merge by name. Unknown site routing stays unassigned, without other tenants' customer text.

Measure fitting signals, actual responses, specifically qualified needs, fulfilment/partner acceptance, paid results and costs separately. Follow the plan's NOT RUN calibration scenarios before enabling an adapter. Self-calibration may improve a versioned reply strategy; it cannot grant rights, change budgets/mandates/current business facts or rewrite public packages. Document plan vs implemented vs verified capability honestly.
