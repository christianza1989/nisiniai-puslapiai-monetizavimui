# Intent and original value

## Decide URL ownership before writing

Use the existing URL inventory and business scope as anchors. A keyword-family record keeps exact observed query, market/device/date, candidate variants, any measured volume/source/window, representative returned URLs and observed page types. Keep unqueried variants distinct from observations. For Lithuanian, retain diacritics and grammatical forms; compare actual results rather than treating singular/plural as automatically distinct demand.

When useful, calculate URL overlap for two controlled organic samples: intersection size / union size, with returned sample sizes and normalized URLs. Remove tracking parameters cautiously but do not merge materially different paths. Domain overlap and URL overlap answer different questions. Similar SERPs support shared intent; different SERPs can reflect timing, freshness, personalization or sampling depth. Do not enforce a universal 50%/70% splitting threshold. Inspect the actual reader decision, page format and evidence needed.

Choose an existing page if it can answer the job coherently. A new page requires a distinct useful function and a path to discovery. A new budget, city, synonym or recipient label alone is not enough. Different recipients may justify distinct advice when constraints and choices differ; every cross-product recipient × holiday × budget permutation does not deserve an indexable page.

Commercial result sets call for usable offers, catalogues or comparisons. A new editorial site should not pretend to be a marketplace. Find a research job it can serve better and offer a real next step; when genuinely authorized commerce exists, use the actual product/offer registry. Foreign competitors suggest formats and mechanisms, not Lithuanian demand or our fulfilment facts.

## Contribution brief

Document the direct answer, mechanism/selection criteria, worked example, situations where the advice changes, current source needs and concrete original contribution. Choose what fits the niche: a verified comparison, tested tool, original images/process, documented experiment, current primary dataset, real expert review, or a transparent practical selection method. A paraphrase of the top ten pages, a made-up test or a generated image of a supposedly tested product is not original evidence.

For gifts, useful axes can be recipient interests/relationship, occasion, budget, deadline, format, personalization and experience. Separate verified product facts from editorial suitability judgments. Examples should state when they are illustrative. Availability, price, fulfilment, photo/music rights and voucher terms need current appropriate evidence; they do not transfer between suppliers or our domains.

For technical/high-stakes niches, use current primary documentation and scope any real specialist review. Unknown consequential claims remain private or are omitted. Do not replace substantial answers with a universal list of questions to ask a provider.

## Link graph and metadata

Pick a root that genuinely includes its children. Child → root, root → child and selected sibling/cross-cluster links must help discovery or the next decision. Store anchor, target ID/path, reader reason and public readiness. A planned link is not a live href; no self-links, all-to-all site networks or unreviewed external-looking owned targets.

Create an accurate unique title and useful description after deciding the page job. Brand naming, query language and H1 should be natural. Do not add every variant, arbitrary word-count targets or a year merely for freshness. Review rendered snippets and dates where data exists. Acceptance requires a concrete sufficient answer and verified facts, not a specific number of headings, FAQs, sources or words.

## Prioritization

Show a few interpretable dimensions rather than an opaque score: business fit, observed relevant demand, gap in useful answers, capacity for distinctive evidence, season/dependency urgency, and effort. Identify what observation would change the order. Complete useful connected groups when ready; maintenance and seasonal review can occur later without artificial publishing quotas.

Official grounding: [Google helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) supports original reader value and truthful experience; [Google AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) emphasizes non-commodity material and cautions against pages for every query variation. [Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) cover scaled low-value content, doorway pages, hidden content and manipulative linking. These principles do not imply an article-count formula or ranking guarantee.
