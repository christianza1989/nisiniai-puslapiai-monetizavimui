## Operator use and rollback

Open the paid order in login.memocasting.lt/admin, prepare its actual package plan, then use each numbered package's quote and preparation controls. Each command has its own carrier price, provider ID and complete PDF. The order becomes labelled after every planned slot is registered. Extra carrier cost belongs to the seller and does not change the saved buyer total. One actually measured combined box remains an alternative. Unknown carrier outcomes must be reconciled, never blindly repurchased.

Do not revert to a backend before0029 while indexed commands exist: it can mark a partially shipped order complete. Keep0029/history and the slot completion guards. A scoped policy reversal uses all_parcels with the current full-config SHA; do not restore the old store snapshot over later owner changes. UI-only rollback may use frontend603ce8b6 with current bindings. Retain the current backend or apply a forward repair. No rollback of customer payments, coupon usage, mail or cron.
