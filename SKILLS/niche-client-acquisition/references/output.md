# Acquisition handoff

Read only the mode needed. Keep site files private; no prospect / correspondence exports into public content packages. These are output contracts, not new runtime schemas.

## Site plan

`sites/<siteId>/ACQUISITION.md`: date / BUSINESS version; buyer, payer, paid result and actual capability; buyer vs supplier vs referral roles; inspected channel sources and usage rights; chosen audience / offer / signal / exclusions; contact / provider mode; experiment time, research and spend limits; true qualification / outcome metrics; attribution dependencies; continue / revise / stop conditions; implementation status / unknown facts. Distinguish proposed experiment parameters from market forecasts.

For a domain-only build this can be a brief actionable private plan. It does not require a mailing engine, contact database, a paid account or a new owner interview.

## Prospect or signal record

Return only needed fields: siteId, supported organization key / role, source URL, source date and inspectedAt, concise factual signal, expiry or known timing, fit / intent / recency reasons, contactability and fulfilment status, minimum permitted contact with its own source if requested, exclusions, nextAction. No made-up contacts / personal details. Do not imply a researched public request has become our customer's enquiry.

## Draft or exchange

Return siteId, requested recipient / thread reference, factual offer and personalization sources, subject / body, one next step, action mode, unresolved delivery / factual dependencies. Clearly state private draft vs actual sending. Use available authorized tools for actual requested reversible updates; do not claim nonexistent integrations.

Read the complete final subject/body for every exported recipient, including manual drafts assembled outside the model runner. Record the version/content hash and concrete review result privately; list size, schema PASS, source fit and prompt instructions do not establish message quality. A usable letter connects a verified recipient fact to the current offer's practical purpose, uses natural language, asks one proportionate question and includes a simple way to decline. Do not make unknown price, current traffic, availability, qualification or planned functionality sound confirmed.

Research notes and exclusions are operator metadata, not recipient text. Street/city corrections, missing-field observations and source-rights warnings must not be copied into prose. An unspecified provider stays in further research until its services are known; one multi-location organization is not several unrelated prospects. Keep exact failed originals and revised drafts separate with their original source expiry; copy correction does not renew evidence.

Report content review by the operator and independent model separately from acquisition engine `draft_ready`, source/contact/transport authorization and delivery. Where the owner requires personal review, inspect every final initial letter and follow-up before the authorized batch; a model grade alone is insufficient. Refusal, wrong recipient, silence, duplicate organization, stale evidence, uncertain submission and changed offer belong in the applicable calibration/gate evidence. No finite case suite proves zero errors or that a campaign is welcome. Publish only redacted counts/failures/hashes; keep organization, correspondence and exact copy packets private.

An unresolved privacy/rights request must show its actual next owner action. A no-outbound `privacy_handoff` binds the inbound quote and holds/stops marketing without claiming publication/deletion verification or Case delivery. The local lab receipt explicitly has `handoff_executed=false`; do not report the request as fulfilled. Refusal suppression remains required even if the generated privacy reply fails provenance validation.

Conditional permission such as "contact us when the prices are confirmed" is a held next action, not current outreach consent. Preserve the exact `resume_condition_quote` privately and show `resume_authorized=false`; no additional letter is needed to acknowledge the condition. Retained terminal refusal/complaint history cannot be silently converted into a new conversation during replay.

## Experiment review

Record investigated, fitting, contactable, reached, replied, specifically qualified, partner-accepted, fulfilled and paid counts with evidence / denominators; buyer / partner separation; bounce / refusal / complaint; actual time, AI / data / transport costs; source and offer version. Zero qualified needs is not zero acquisition cost. Recommend the next bounded change; don't assert profitability from list size or synthetic tests.
