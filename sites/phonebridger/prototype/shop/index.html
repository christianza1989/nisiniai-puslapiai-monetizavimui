What does the app do?+

Move your mouse through a chosen screen edge to control your own Android apps. Type with your PC keyboard, send supported Google links and listen to supported phone audio through your PC.

Does the app need a holder?+

No. App only works with your existing phone setup. Optional holders change the physical arrangement, not the app features.

Can I connect three phones by USB?+

The beta supports one physical USB phone at a time. Other paired phones can use Wi-Fi. USB takes priority automatically after its one-time setup.

Is this a subscription?+

The planned offer is a one-time V1 licence for one Windows PC and up to three Android phones. A future paid major version would be a separate offer. Paid licensing and checkout are not active in this beta.

What about shipping and compatibility?+

Hardware orders and shipping are not open. Ask us about your phone, case and laptop before choosing a mount. Final dimensions, attachments and fulfilment details will be confirmed before sale.
