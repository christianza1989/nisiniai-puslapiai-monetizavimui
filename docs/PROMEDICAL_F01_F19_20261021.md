## Final behavior

One eligible projection supplies the visible Promedical → category → product breadcrumb,
`BreadcrumbList`, catalog filter, related anchors and LLM full export. Catalog relations are independent
of the product's editorial links. Every suggested product belongs to the **exact displayed category**.
If a leaf has fewer than two other public products, use the first available registered catalog parent
or other corroborated category with enough products. Keep the same choice across every surface.

Related order: category; 2–3 nearest products, exact model/complete numeric-stem variants first;
then explicitly incoming eligible guides, newest publication first. Deduplicate; no self links; cap five.
The new-guide priority is tested even when five slots were already occupied. Similar model spelling
is a ranking hint, never a technical compatibility claim. Different numeric models/years remain separate.

All 1408 catalog products pass strict membership checks. Two genuinely sparse products, text
**1874 and 1875**, each have only one other product in their catalog group. They show that one;
the template does not invent another recommendation. Of 1408, 1358 show three product recommendations,
48 show two and two show one. The rewritten inventory's sparse IDs: 1874, 1875.

New category publication uses registered sidecar assignments/current path ancestors. New incoming guides
use ordinary approval, date and publication-pause rules; product content is not rewritten. Clock-bound
tests exercise before/after, revocation and a held guide. They are isolated projection fixtures;
**no real guide/category was approved or published** as an experiment.

Root/category result pages now expose all valid result-page hrefs inside a native disclosure.
This is needed for F19: outgoing links from a product alone do not shorten inbound discovery.
