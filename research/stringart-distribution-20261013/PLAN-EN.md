# MemoryString: business and distribution plan

13 October 2026 | MB Pinet | English working plan, version 4

## Recommended business

Build one brand with four connected revenue lines: finished photo portraits, equipment distribution, consumables and DIY kits, and a supported operating package for local production partners. Our Lithuanian pilot studio would validate production, train partners and establish the quality standard. The commercial ambition is principal EU distribution, followed by selected international markets.

The strongest supplier proposition is a repeatable route to equipment and consumables sales: local studios, training, fulfilment, marketing and a system that reports real demand. An impressive demonstration supports that discussion; it does not replace measured sales or create exclusive rights.

MemoryString is the owner's candidate name alongside their existing MemoryCasting hand-casting kit business. Trademark clearance is pending; a separate MemoryString Mac photo/video app already exists. Keep the two product lines distinct while testing shared gift campaigns.

## Current position

- We have a supplier quotation and supplied PDFs, images and videos. No machine has been purchased or physically tested.
- Hardware, software ownership, conformity documents, territory rights and true landed costs remain unresolved.
- The partner network is a proposed business. Ten demonstration partners are synthetic, with 16 machines and 825 kits in the planned dataset.
- The sales website and full partner-platform implementation are paused at the owner's request. The separate creator app is authorized, Android first, English throughout. A local test APK has compiled successfully; physical-phone and frame acceptance remain pending.
- Supplier correspondence is a draft. No supplier message, purchase, campaign, domain migration or public deployment has been performed.

## Immediate sequence

Confirm the manufacturer and exact product; negotiate software/territory/DIY terms; inspect conformity and logistics; validate one machine and kit; demonstrate the system honestly; test one sales market; then recruit one or two real partners. Request principal-distributor status against agreed performance milestones rather than promising a global network before a pilot.

Production receives the exact approved file and layout version. Duplicate payment/shipment events must not create duplicate production or commissions.

# 14. Conformity, rights and customer data

Before importing/using/selling the machine, confirm the exact SKU and applicable conformity route, responsible parties, model-specific declaration, supporting technical evidence and instructions. A CE mark in a photograph is not the evidence package.

The EU machinery framework generally transitions on 20 January 2027 from Directive 2006/42/EC to Regulation 2023/1230; assess the relevant placement date and obligations for the actual configuration. Own-brand marketing or modifications can create manufacturer responsibilities. Preserve original equipment identity initially; assess OEM separately. [Machinery Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1230/oj)

Assess moving parts, pinch points, pins, emergency stopping, safe thread changes, electrical/EMC requirements and radio rules where relevant. Obtain warranty scope, parts supply, fault process and transport/labour responsibilities. Supplier HS 8447.9090.00 is a starting proposal, not an accepted EU customs ruling.

Trademark/design/patent review is separate from software licensing. RingString's public patent claims do not establish our freedom to operate. Validate our own kit design, instructions, label, hanging and package. Different MemoryCasting chemicals and string-art hardware have distinct requirements.

Keep customer photos private, isolate partners, define deletion and retention, and identify controller/processor roles. Confirm whether supplier software sends images/files outside the EEA and the lawful transfer arrangement. Wi-Fi or a job number does not prove EU-only processing. [Commission SCC information](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/standard-contractual-clauses-scc_en)

Separate withdrawal rules for genuinely personalized goods from remedies for faulty goods; assess standard DIY kits separately. Determine VAT/OSS, importer, packaging and applicable electronics obligations for each actual sales market. [Consumer returns](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/guarantees-returns/index_en.htm), [VAT OSS](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/index_en).

These are due-diligence work items. Company registration, bank, conformity, trademark clearance and physical safety are not verified by this plan.
