# Adapt the process to the customer job

This is a decision aid, not a catalogue to copy into every site. Select relevant patterns and discard the rest. Business hypotheses are not commitments.

Apply these patterns only after the business choice: named customer/payer, paid outcome, revenue path and truthful phase-one intent signal. The crafts example below illustrates cluster structure for an explicitly selected craft/digital-product business; it is not a default offer for a domain or evidence that an ideas portal is commercially viable. Content calendars support the selected business rather than determine it.

| Niche pattern | Useful intent families | Calendar signals to investigate | Evidence/offer boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local services: websites, repairs, blinds | Scope, request preparation, options, cost drivers, maintenance | Actual customer planning cycles; seasons only when relevant | Real service area and capacity required; no city variants without distinct local value |
| Technical products: tractor tyres, materials | Markings, compatibility data, selection tradeoffs, safe maintenance, inquiry checklist | Preparation before local fieldwork/building seasons, storage after use | Manufacturer model/specification context; no universal fitment, pressure or invented stock |
| Crafts and gift ideas | Materials, skill level, finished project instructions, alternatives | Relevant holidays, school/project preparation, verified movable dates | An idea page must enable making something; promised printables need usable checked files |
| Gift selection/editorial centres | Recipient needs, occasion, format tradeoffs, full budget, personalization input, purchase deadline and use conditions | Verified occasions and preparation/delivery windows; evergreen choice questions remain evergreen | Distinguish selection from DIY craft instructions; current supplier/product evidence, no stereotyped recipients, fabricated tests or promised checkout |
| B2B/digital: reporting, automation | Workflow diagnosis, input checklist, worked example, option comparison | Real budget/reporting cycles; industry dates if evidenced | Sample data labelled illustrative; no fake client savings, integrations or case studies |
| Travel/accommodation | Trip planning, accessibility, transport, seasonal activities | Local seasonality, actual event dates, reservation lead time | Do not imply our property/availability exists; original visit claims need evidence |
| Construction/home improvement | Requirements, choices, request checklist, upkeep | Local conditions, project planning before execution | Regulations and technical constraints verified; no invented qualifications or structural advice |
| Regulated/high consequence topics | General decision literacy, documents needed, official process | Jurisdiction-specific deadlines, current rules | Avoid personalized medical/legal/financial conclusions; unresolved consequential guidance stays private |

## Example: tractor tyre information site

Hypothesis: help an owner collect the information needed for a tyre selection inquiry. Unknown supplier relationships and inventory remain unknown.

- Evergreen selection root: requirements for choosing tractor tyres.
- Distinct supports: reading sidewall markings; information required to check rim/fitment; why load/speed affect manufacturer pressure tables; storage checklist.
- Spring preparation timing may be useful in Lithuania, but fieldwork dates and pressure are not universal.
- A useful contribution is a request checklist containing tractor model, current markings, axle/configuration, rim details and operating conditions, with explanation of why each matters.
- Never add Christmas discount tyres, exact bar recommendations or a shop/product offer merely to fill the calendar.

## Example: craft instructions site

Hypothesis: beginner-friendly project guides. Existing downloads: none.

- Neutral root: choosing a seasonal craft by time, materials and skill level.
- Distinct supports: Christmas paper decoration instructions; a Valentine's card technique; an Easter paper project when the date is verified.
- Christmas is a child or separate cluster, never the root of unrelated holidays.
- Publish preparation guides before their relevant occasion. Verify Easter's year/date; do not carry an old year's calendar forward.
- Either deliver an actual tested printable or remove "download template" from title/body. Material quantities and steps must allow a reader to complete the proposed project.

## Transfer to a new niche

Infer a narrow initial customer job from the brief, label assumptions and research what readers cannot presently decide. Create the complete bounded question map, then choose the smallest useful first release group. Do not substitute that group for the full researched map. A technical-products checklist cannot be adapted to a reporting service by substituting nouns; the decisions, evidence, examples, seasonal triggers and next action must change.

Reconcile the current and confirmed future category/product registry with this map. A platform procedure, provider-specific variant and editorial question are separate objects. Do not create one article for every category × recipient × budget × city combination. Map each distinct decision to an existing section/revision or a new URL with its own answer and evidence. Select the site's actual next-action facets rather than inheriting another niche's filters.
