Who prepares the PhoneBridger guides

Clear instructions, traceable sources and honest limits.

The organizational attribution for these guides is MB Pinet, the operator named in the shared project configuration. PhoneBridger’s guide contact is hello@phonebridger.com. We do not list a fictional individual expert, customer test or independent certification.

How these guides are prepared

AI assists research, drafting and layout. Product instructions are checked against the current release documentation; platform requirements are checked against linked primary Android or Microsoft sources. An agent’s editorial review is not an independent human expert review.

What the illustrations mean

Original contextual illustrations show a plausible desk, cable or permission theme. They do not document a customer installation, stocked product, exact device compatibility or a measured test. Precise controls and diagrams are provided as editable text and vector graphics. Required third-party credits are retained where applicable.

Versions and dates

These guides were prepared and checked on 6 October 2026 for the beta described on Downloads. This private preview is not evidence of public publication. Before launch, the real first publication date and any later meaningful review must be recorded; changing a calendar date alone does not update the guidance.

Scope and corrections

Use the guides to understand setup and feature limits. They do not replace Android’s own permission explanation, your organization’s policy or the manufacturer’s hardware requirements. Send the page URL, the inaccurate sentence and a supporting source to hello@phonebridger.com. Do not send passwords, pairing codes or private messages.

What we do not claim

There is no measured latency guarantee, supported-device certification, independent product review or proof of demand in these guides. Paid orders, production customer accounts and native file dragging are not activated by the website preview.
