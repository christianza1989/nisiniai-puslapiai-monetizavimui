# Client content plan

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Standalone offline HTML/CSS/JavaScript, explicitly requested by the user as an HTML file to send to a client. This is a planning artifact, not the production website, content publisher or booking implementation.

## Users

The user presents a three-villa website project to the client. The client must understand topic coverage, each page's reader question and original contribution, page relationships, fact dependencies and the staged release.

## Product Purpose

Explore the planned SEO/GEO content system and leave downloadable local comments. Success means the recipient can navigate the topic map, inspect a full page brief and send their comments back without signing into a hosted product.

## Evidence on Hand

Grand Vila full audit and 51 sitemap URLs, Vilaitė32 and Vila Pasaka33 sitemap URLs; nine additional source pages reviewed. Source facts, dates and uncertainties are in ../PILNAS-PLANAS.md and ../artifacts/. Search-volume, precise LT/device SERPs and GEO performance are not measured. New canonical domain unknown.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Single transferable file, responsive down to320CSSpx, keyboard controls, self-contained data and fonts, no external services or submissions. Local notes export works; notes are not a formal content approval. Stable planning IDs, proposed paths and link reasons are included. Plans are distinct from drafts, approved revisions, deployed content and measured search results. No fake authority score, prices or availability.

## Product Principles

Specific decision per page, useful connections, truthful sources and business facts, readable dense information, clear actions. The author chooses ordinary presentation decisions under project adaptation; no additional owner interview is needed for facts already settled by the explicit brief. Unconfirmed business facts remain open.
