Before you start.A few clear details.

The beta is available. Paid orders are not open yet.

Current beta

PhoneBridger connects a Windows PC to Android phones. This beta is under development and has the compatibility and feature limits described on the downloads and help pages. Keep your own files backed up and review permissions before enabling a feature.

Planned store offer

Displayed USD prices are planned launch prices. They are not an open checkout or a confirmed shipping quote. The intended offer is a one-time V1 licence for one Windows PC and up to three Android phones, with optional hardware. Paid activation is not implemented in the beta.

Shipping, taxes, seller details, hardware specifications and sales/refund policies will be confirmed before a purchase is available. A future paid major version would be a separate offer. The software-box image is an illustration of a digital download, not a shipped software box.

Saving or asking about a setup

Saving a setup records a choice in your browser. Preparing an email creates a draft in your email app. Neither action places an order, reserves stock, charges a payment or establishes a shipping commitment.

Website and account preview

The live demo uses sample data. Local preview accounts exist only to verify the email/password flow; they do not grant a paid licence or creator role. No creator dashboard or commission programme is activated here.

Questions

For compatibility, beta or planned-store questions, contact hello@phonebridger.com.
