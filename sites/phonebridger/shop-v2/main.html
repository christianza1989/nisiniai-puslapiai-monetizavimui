Talk to us ↗
Do I need a holder to use the app?+

No. Use an existing phone stand or another suitable arrangement. The optional magnetic holders change where your phones sit, not what the app can do.

Is the app a subscription?+

This is a one-time V1 licence for one Windows PC and up to three Android phones. A future paid major version is a separate offer. The beta remains free to try; buying does not unlock extra beta features. After confirmed payment, your purchase and licence record appear in your account.

What arrives with a setup?+

Software is downloaded, not supplied in a physical software box. A hardware bundle includes your selected number of foldable magnetic holders in the chosen finish, sent directly by our supplier. The software-box image is a digital-product illustration; no physical software box is shipped.

Where do you ship, and what does delivery cost?+

We offer free worldwide shipping to destinations available at Checkout. Hardware is sent directly by our supplier after we arrange your order. Delivery time depends on destination and the carrier; tracking appears in your account after dispatch. Import duties or local charges, if applicable, are separate from our shipping charge.

Can I return a holder or request a refund?+

App-only purchases can be cancelled within 14 days of purchase. A hardware bundle can be returned within 14 days of receiving it. Ordinary change-of-mind return postage is paid by you. See our purchase terms for the withdrawal form, instructions and faulty-goods rights.

Who will handle the payment?+

Your seller is MB Memocasting. Available purchases are paid on Stripe-hosted Checkout. No card details are entered or stored on this shop page.
