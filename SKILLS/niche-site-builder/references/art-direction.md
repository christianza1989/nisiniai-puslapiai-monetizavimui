# Art direction for autonomous niche sites

Use for a new visual identity or a substantial redesign. A narrow styling repair should preserve the established identity and skip concept exploration. The owner has delegated ordinary decisions: make them, record the evidence and continue. Design quality must be visible in the rendered work.

## Build a useful visual brief

Separate business evidence from craft references. Niche shops reveal questions, vocabulary and needed information. Strong contemporary references reveal composition, visual hierarchy and finish. Research both, with screenshots of actual pages where available. Siteinspire and the public portions of Landbook can identify references; inspect the linked website yourself. A dramatic studio portfolio is not automatically the right model for agricultural procurement.

For each adopted reference note the URL, date, screenshot, viewport, precise transferable decision and what does not fit our audience. Examples of meaningful decisions: a compact navigation that prioritizes the selection task; a product silhouette that anchors scale; a typographic index that makes guides scannable; a comparison table that replaces marketing cards. "Looks premium" is not a usable observation. Preserve source copyrights; do not transplant another site's copy, logo, images or complete composition.

Define an audience-specific thesis: who is arriving, what uncertainty matters, what the page helps them do, and which visual idea makes that task memorable. A farmer decoding a sidewall, a couple choosing a gift, and a business ordering a website need different emphasis. Do not assign all three one agency-style landing template.

For this network apply [independent identities](design-diversity.md): inspect the nearest existing niche screenshots as well as outside references. Plan brand/section/asset roles and evaluate a useful small tool before generation. Selection must address cross-site resemblance, not only choose the best of internally similar concepts.

## Compare concepts before committing

For a genuinely open identity, normally compare two or three materially different compositions. Vary the first viewport, section rhythm, information density, image relationship and type hierarchy. Three colour swaps of one split hero are one concept. Think beyond the category's obvious style while keeping the customer's task immediately understandable.

Use actual Lithuanian headings, meaningful body copy, true actions and available visuals in lightweight local HTML/CSS studies, or use image-generated comps when they materially help art direction. Keep experiments in development output and outside publicly imported packages. Raster comps are references; they are never a screenshot embedded as the real homepage. Their text and demonstration features have no authority over approved content.

Inspect the studies visually at desktop and mobile. Select autonomously when delegated, using audience identification, clarity of the next action, distinctive composition, coherent hierarchy and feasible performance. Document one reason to choose the winner and one real compromise; qualitative evidence is more useful than invented precision or a pretend consensus. Do not claim another reviewer assessed it unless that review actually occurred.

## Persist the chosen system

Use `sites/<siteId>/DESIGN.md` as the niche's durable record. Keep it compact enough for another agent to implement consistently:

- Active domain, audience, page modes and actual next action.
- Thesis and reference evidence; familiar defaults that were deliberately rejected.
- Chosen concept, selection rationale, desktop/mobile first-viewport composition and section rhythm.
- Palette with roles and hex values; font sources/licenses, Lithuanian glyph support, weights and type scale.
- Grid, reading width, spacing scale, image framing, borders/radii and component states.
- A few signature treatments tied to real content; what stays quiet on reading/contact pages.
- Header, footer, guide, form and responsive behavior; motion only when it improves orientation or feedback.
- Screenshots, visual findings, fixes, measured technical results and unresolved limits.

The core can share mechanics without forcing one skin on every domain. A guide inherits that niche's identity but prioritizes reading. A local studio's admin UI prioritizes efficient operation. Do not let a design loader's repository-wide context overwrite domain-specific decisions.

## Build with restraint and material specificity

Typography is an active layout decision: check actual line breaks, width, weight, rhythm and Lithuanian diacritics in the browser. Changing Arial to a fashionable font does not rescue weak composition. A common font is legitimate when deliberately set and appropriate; do not turn a detector's font warning into a universal ban.

Use hierarchy through placement, scale, whitespace and useful imagery before adding containers. Cards belong where items are meaningfully parallel, not around every paragraph. Repeated eyebrow labels, arrows, identical three-column grids and identical section spacing need a reason. They are legitimate devices, but a page built entirely from these reflexes should be reconsidered.

Create visuals from the subject's actual needs: an original illustration, a carefully framed material close-up, an understandable diagram, or a true comparison. A tractor selection diagram must not invent compatible sizes or engineering limits. Generated product-looking imagery must not imply real stock, authorized brands or completed work. Record provenance; optimize the final assets.

For each proposed visual, name the reader question it answers and the approved evidence it uses. An atmospheric hero can establish character; the rest of the page should develop the customer's task rather than repeat promotional rows. Where explanation is the offer, consider an annotated notation, selectable worksheet, comparison or other subject-specific teaching artefact. Choose only what clarifies this niche; do not require a diagram on every site or decorate empty information. Reuse one authoritative explanation across relevant pages rather than introducing independent hardcoded facts.

Before shipping, inspect the middle and end without the hero. If all sections are the same heading/paragraph grid, decide which questions really need parallel items and which need a different reading structure. Repeated guide destinations need not each have a large promotional section. Keep instructions visible after input begins, preserve useful section navigation on mobile, and make identical action labels lead to the same contextual task. Secondary text and form controls must remain comfortable at the measured narrow viewport, including consent, caveats and image disclosures; a small-font desktop design is not a mobile layout.

Give a page a coherent central idea rather than collecting every fashionable section. Do not fix a generic site with gratuitous gradients, a cursor toy, forced GSAP pinning, a bento grid or a hero video. Purposeful motion can help, but the content and primary action must remain available without it, with reduced motion and keyboard use supported.

## Visual acceptance

Assess screenshots before automated findings, using explicit evidence:

| Question | Evidence to record |
|---|---|
| Does the first viewport belong to this niche? | Identify the meaningful visual decision; compare actual nearest-network screenshots and test whether another niche could replace the nouns/images without substantive change |
| Is there a clear hierarchy? | Title, supporting explanation and primary action are readable without competing badges or decoration |
| Is the composition intentional? | Alignment, image crop, line breaks, scale and density relate to the chosen concept and reference quality |
| Does the full page have useful rhythm? | Section structure changes with the customer's question; repeated cards are justified by genuinely parallel items |
| Is mobile designed as a composition? | Actual narrow screenshot, useful content order, visible next step, readable headings and controls; no overflow hidden merely to conceal defects |
| Does the identity extend beyond the hero? | Guide, contact/form, navigation and footer visibly belong to the same site |
| Is craft supported by actual function? | Real links and form states, true facts, accessible focus/contrast, images correctly rendered |

Automated contrast, overflow, detector and Lighthouse findings complement this review. None measures originality, audience fit or conversion. Record blockers and repair them together, then confirm once. Further iteration needs a specific unresolved problem, not dissatisfaction with a numeric style score.
