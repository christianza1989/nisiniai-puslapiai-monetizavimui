# MemoryString

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Android phone and an offline browser creator using the same controller, solver and local assets.

## Stack

Browser JavaScript, a pinned MIT Web Worker solver and IndexedDB. Android Java Activity/WebView, native TextToSpeech and system document picker. Node 24 builds the asset without npm dependencies. Android SDK 35 and Gradle 8.9 build the local test APK. No server or SQLite belongs to this current creator package.

## Users

DIY customers prepare a photo and follow physical pin instructions. The owner and future partners evaluate previews and kit usability. A separate partner business platform is the next authorized task after the app, in a requested new chat.

## Product Purpose

Convert a local photo into a continuous thread sequence, show the current connection, optionally dictate it in English, and preserve progress through an exact frame-bound project.

## Operating Context

Android first, a phone near a numbered frame. Proposed 42/50/60 cm presets are untested with a real kit. The owner has a supplier quotation and no tested machine. Hands may be occupied; text, repeat, back and next remain available without voice.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Cropping/pan/zoom/rotation, brightness/contrast, monochrome/four-colour preview, distinct colour tie-on paths, saved projects, English device voices, automatic pacing and JSON backup. Only installed offline voices are selected. This package has no INTERNET/microphone/broad-storage permission, partner authentication, QR activation or accepted machine export. Android compilation and physical-phone acceptance are separate states.

## Brand Commitments

MemoryString is a candidate related to the owner's MemoryCasting hand-casting kits. An existing MemoryString Mac app makes clearance a real pending item. Competitor screenshots are workflow references; their photographs, UI and identity are not licensed assets in this app.

## Evidence on Hand

Dated supplier/market research, original private supplier files, the GitHub assessment and pinned solver notice. An explicitly selected AI photo demonstrates computation; it is not a customer portrait or hardware result. Source-bound verification is in mobile/ACCEPTANCE.md.

## Product Principles

- Keep each full sequence tied to its declared layout and algorithm.
- Save completed progress, with export backups and independent imported IDs.
- Advance only after narration completion and the chosen interval.
- Pause obsolete narration when the user changes steps or leaves the app.
- Describe actual preview/build states without implying physical acceptance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

English interface and narration, readable text alongside optional speech, visible keyboard focus, controls at least 44 px high, responsive wrapping and tested narrow/text-enlargement states. No blanket WCAG certification or physical-device acceptance claim.

## Partner operations — separate local module

The owner's 14 October next-phase request is now implemented under [platform/](platform/README.md): a persistent English operator/partner/supplier workspace with synthetic stock, portrait/DIY fulfilment, machine service and referral accounting. Its one [current plan](platform/PLAN.md) and [acceptance](platform/ACCEPTANCE.md) define the entities, permissions, transitions and evidence. It extends the same identity and reads the original prepared asset kit and exact JSON validator; the Android creator stack and package remain unchanged. The local Node24/SQLite adapter is not production auth, a public retail website or a physical manufacturer interface.
