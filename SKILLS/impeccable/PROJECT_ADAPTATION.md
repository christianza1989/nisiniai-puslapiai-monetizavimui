# Impeccable in the niche-site network

The owner has delegated ordinary niche research, offer hypotheses, visual direction, concept selection and implementation. Project `AGENTS.md` and the user's instructions remain authoritative. This adaptation applies to the shared niche-site projects, not unrelated work.

Read [the shared project skill contract](../PROJECT_CONTRACT.md) and use the actual active-domain paths. Upstream examples of approvals, external services, global PRODUCT/DESIGN files and delegation do not expand the authorized task. A concept decision is an agent decision when delegated; it is not human approval.

## Autonomous design decisions

Use the supplied brief, documented facts and research to resolve ordinary aesthetic and layout choices yourself. Upstream interview, confirmation, decision-page and concept-lock steps become documented agent decisions when the user has delegated them. Do not stop for a font, palette, framework choice already settled by the shared core, or a concept approval. Keep genuinely unknown business facts explicitly unknown. Never describe an agent-selected direction as human approved.

Use real tools for concept seeds, screenshots, generation and checks; never fabricate their output. Keep experiments proportional to the assignment. For a new identity or major redesign, compare a small number of materially different visual directions and choose based on audience fit, clarity, distinctive composition and implementation cost. A small refinement inherits the established direction.

Upstream specialist/handoff playbooks may require a human asset checkpoint or parallel reviewers. In this network routine concept/asset review remains delegated; record the agent's judgement and actual evidence. Delegate only when the user's or applicable project/tool instructions authorize it and the tools exist. Otherwise do the permitted review sequentially and do not call it independent. Do not manufacture an approval request or spawn agents simply to fulfil an upstream template.

## Review and implementation

For this network use the builder's [design-diversity contract](../niche-site-builder/references/design-diversity.md). Compare the actual nearest niche designs before selection and after implementation, and plan brand/sections/image roles before generation. A seed, token set, clean detector or independent renderer is not evidence of independence. Do not let upstream editorial/minimal examples become a network-wide house style. Small customer tools are permitted within phase one when useful and verifiable; no operational capability may be invented.

Use `niche-site-builder` as the domain workflow and Impeccable for visual invention, hierarchy, typography and finishing. Preserve host isolation, draft visibility, SEO/GEO, contact attribution and demand measurement. Build semantic, server-rendered HTML in the existing architecture. A design comp is a development artifact; any mockup text, generated imagery or demonstration interaction must be replaced by true content and working behavior before shipping.

Use the existing browser tools for screenshots. Evaluate design specificity and composition before reading detector findings so a clean scan cannot stand in for visual judgement. Then run the detector and appropriate accessibility/performance checks. Respect actual tool instructions and authorization for delegation; an autonomous workflow does not grant permission for arbitrary third-party messaging. If assessment is sequential in one context, say so rather than calling it independent. The user's delegated design decisions do not authorize fabricated business facts, paid services or unrelated deployment.

Inspect in bounded passes: one combined desktop/mobile review, one batch of meaningful repairs, then confirmation. Further rounds require a concrete unresolved defect. Record screenshot paths, the chosen direction and its compromises. Lighthouse and heuristic scores do not prove conversion, top rankings or designer-level craft.

The upstream two-pass ceiling limits arbitrary polishing, not repair of a demonstrated critical defect or completion of a required acceptance check. Record the reason for an additional targeted verification instead of stopping with a known unresolved requirement or repeatedly rerolling aesthetics. Installed references about tool prices/licenses are dated research; verify current terms before introducing a dependency. Existing free local/native components are adequate when they satisfy the task.
