News

UAB "Fasado pastoliai" specializes in the scaffolding hire market. Our company hires German facade and modular caned scaffolding (the so called contour scaffolding) and sells new and used scaffolding of the following companies: MJ, PLETTAC, LAYHER, RUX. Our company offers a full complex of services: scaffolding rent, their delivery to the object, their installation, de-installation and collection from the object. The equipment we work with is certified. We work both with legal and physical entities and we are flexible and considerate to every single client. 

Our mission is long-term sustainable partnership.

The company has branches in Vilnius, Alytus and Marijampolė and equipment warehouses in Vilnius and Alytus.
