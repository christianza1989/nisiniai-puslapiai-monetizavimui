# Independent identities on one technical core

Use before concept selection, asset generation and substantial redesign in this network. The owner's 2026-10-01 review identified visual convergence despite separate renderers. Share publishing, SEO, media processing, forms and measurement. Do not inherit another niche's presentation as the default brand. Honest operator identity and the required attribution remain visible; visual independence does not conceal ownership.

## Compare with the network, not only your own concepts

Inspect the nearest existing niche designs using actual desktop/mobile homepage, guide and index screenshots. Prefer sites with similar audiences or material imagery, plus a recent build when useful. Record which artifacts/versions were inspected. If unavailable, record the gap instead of claiming a comparison. Read each relevant DESIGN, but screenshots determine what was actually built. Do not modify another site during comparison.

In the new site's DESIGN, record a short comparison: opening composition, navigation/brand treatment, section rhythm, image language, typography/density and useful customer interaction. Identify its nearest visual neighbour and the meaningful decisions that distinguish the new site. A different font/palette/subject in the same composition is weak differentiation. Random colours and unfamiliar controls are not a remedy. A shared conventional component is fine when its customer benefit is clear; judge the combined impression.

Before selecting concepts, define the reader's task and an identity thesis. Compare genuinely different ways to perform that task, including their image placement and mobile reading order. If all concepts resemble an existing niche, revise the concept family before generating final media. A random seed or novelty score does not establish distinctiveness.

## References must change a decision

Keep offer research and craft research distinct. For each adopted pattern connect the actual final URL, date, viewport and screenshot region to our proposed section or interaction. Record the reader benefit, capability required and adopt/adapt/defer/reject reason. Inspect homepage and relevant inner journey; search snippets or screenshots of another URL cannot establish that page's layout. Cookie-covered screens, unrendered lazy sections and blank areas are limited evidence; capture a useful unobstructed region or choose another reference.

Study strong niche competitors and relevant craft references, then choose a coherent direction rather than aggregating every attractive feature. Do not promise to outrank or outperform every competitor. Name a concrete customer task our implementation should do better and the observable acceptance evidence. Traffic, conversion and commercial advantage remain hypotheses until measured.

## Plan the actual brand and assets before generation

Maintain a section-and-asset plan in DESIGN or a linked site document. For each homepage section and initial page record its question, composition, content source, asset role/ID, aspect ratio, desktop/mobile placement and crop, next action and dependency. A text-only section can explicitly need no image. Do not use a fixed number of images or sections.

Design a niche-specific wordmark or mark, useful favicon and appropriate light/dark/mobile variants. A deliberate type-only identity is valid; do not automatically attach an icon or generated raster logo. Use SVG/CSS/native text for precise scalable identity, with meaningful accessible naming and actual favicon wiring. Never copy a competitor's trademark or imply affiliation.

Give every proposed visual a role: context, comparison, material, sequence, scale, evidence or atmosphere. The first image may establish mood; supporting images should contribute different information. Specify subject, camera angle/distance, light, setting, realism/illustration mode, composition, contrast and mobile focal area. Distinct subject matter alone does not distinguish two image families. Review an overview of the proposed/generated set alongside the nearest site's images before approval. Repeated oak/cream interiors, still-life tools, soft window shadows or empty immaculate scenes are not banned, but cannot become the default for unrelated niches. People are allowed when useful and truthful; never imply generated staff, experts or clients are real.

Generate from the chosen art direction, inspect actual files, then import using MEDIA_CORE. Reject decorative repetition and any malformed technical detail that undermines the page's explanation. Resize variants of one image are not multiple concepts. Preserve provenance and necessary third-party credit; do not add automatic generator labels to original imagery. Avoid portfolios/case-study framing for generated scenes.

## Useful tools can fit phase one

Evaluate whether a small comparison, worksheet, user-input calculation or request preparer solves a recurring researched question better than static prose. Phase one does not prohibit such tools. Choose based on value and trustworthy inputs, not a feature quota or decorative interactivity. Record the decision even when a useful static table is sufficient.

For an included tool define input units, source/date, formula or decision rules, assumptions, result limits, empty/invalid/boundary states, keyboard/mobile behavior and what is stored. Use exact HTML/SVG for explanatory graphics. No fabricated market price, compatible tyre size, safe staircase geometry or binding offer. A sum of user-entered quote items can be truthful without a supplier price database; a live supplier quote cannot. No shopping cart, booking, customer account, hidden lead transmission or supplier automation is introduced through this exception. Keep meaningful content and the contact route available without optional client JavaScript. Verify representative calculations and invalid states; do not merely test that a button renders.

## Accept the final composition

Review actual desktop/mobile first screen, full-page middle/end, guide/index and form against both references and the nearest network designs. Use the seven art-direction criteria, with a reason and screenshot for each judgment. Include cross-site distinction in niche recognition/composition/rhythm; do not invent a separate automated originality metric. Apply the noun-swap check to the whole journey: could the next niche reuse it with only headings, colours and stock-like imagery replaced? If yes, identify and repair the substantive design or information-structure issue before claiming the visual benchmark.

Record similarities honestly. Shared legal/operator information, accessible controls and core mechanics are not defects. The goal is an authored identity serving this customer, not gratuitous novelty. Passing instruction validation, a technical audit, a detector or Lighthouse cannot prove that a later agent will create a 9/10 design on its first attempt.
