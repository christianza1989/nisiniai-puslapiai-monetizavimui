Use case: editorial still life for a Lithuanian beauty appointment decision guide. Page question: What information should a reader confirm before treating a beauty appointment as booked? Image role: suggest preparation and clear agreement, without depicting a real appointment, provider, salon, or booking interface. Create a crisp, contemporary editorial photograph: a simple unmarked paper appointment note, a closed dark notebook and a violet pen on a clean white surface, with a restrained black accent and one pale violet detail. Art direction: deliberate high-contrast palette inspired by #111114, #FFFFFF, #7040E8 and #F1EDFF; precise natural shadows, modern magazine composition, no generic beige spa styling. Composition: landscape 3:2 crop, primary objects grouped near the center with generous clear space; keep the note and pen inside a central mobile-safe area. No readable writing, no logos, no screen, no UI, no person, no identifiable provider, no treatment tools, no claim of availability or service quality, no watermark or model badge.
