# GEO in the normal content pipeline

Use for every plan/draft/review and new page family. This concise contract is loaded in both plan and draft instruction snapshots. The builder/auditor executes integration checks outside the read-only writing job; the writing model cannot approve, deploy, change bots or invent tool calls.

## Content that can support an accurate answer

Give the real reader a distinct, useful answer: explain the choice, constraints and why it differs across relevant cases, then support consequential claims with checked primary evidence and genuine original work. Comparisons, transparent decision methods, examples, actual tools and relevant images can add value. Put answers in normal visible HTML with descriptive headings and readable lists/tables where useful. Do not manufacture first-hand experience, specialists, citations, statistics, partners, testing or community mentions. For consequential health/technical guidance retain real scope/review limitations. No universal paragraph/token/FAQ/citation count, fan-out URL multiplication or special GEO schema applies.

Use the truthful operator/author identity, stable publication/update semantics and actual profile. Link to real relevant answers or eligible service/category/city results through existing resolvers. A planned filter, invented product or empty city page cannot become a recommendation target. Public facts must agree across body, visible attribution, schema, machine exports and business/contact pages. No hidden instructions asking models to cite us or crawler-only claims.

## Automatic outputs and boundaries

The active site's approved package/revision and shared public projection are the only input to HTML, Article/Breadcrumb schema, sitemap and reading exports. `llms.txt` is the conventional optional reading index; `llm.txt` is not our canonical path. `llms-full.txt` is an additional full-text convenience, not a universal engine requirement. Maintain neither by hand or by a second cron/scheduler.

For our network, the existing runtime builds these responses from current eligible content. A new approved release still must be imported, built and actually deployed; merely saving a studio draft never updates the public origin. When a deployed future revision becomes due, the next dynamic response updates its inclusion without another manual TXT edit. On meaningful review/revocation the next installed/deployed revision updates body, links and dates together. Stale CDN/static/service-worker caching must not keep yesterday's eligibility; verify actual no-store or equivalent invalidation/bounded time policy and tenant-aware cache keys.

The index gives the true site name/summary/contact and crawlable canonical reading links; the full output mirrors eligible useful text, genuine author/profile, approved dates, public sources and related links. Source retrieval, approval and scheduled dates must not be falsely labelled publication or human review. Exclude drafts, unapproved/revoked revisions, private provenance/prompts/reviews/customer records and future-only media/links. Reuse current V1/V2 helpers. Do not flatten rich content or fabricate unsupported schema fields. Clean per-page Markdown/alternate/describedby links can be added through a shared adapter when useful; they are optional and require the same publication/privacy gates, not per-niche copies or ranking claims.

## Discovery and current controls

Check public status/canonical, snippet/index eligibility, crawlable navigation, accurate visible schema, responsive reading and real image access. Search Console property inclusion controls, robots groups and CDN/WAF rules can all affect access. Separate search crawlers (Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot) from training bots and user-directed fetchers. Do not equate a GPTBot training preference with OpenAI search eligibility or automatically grant training access. Inspect effective domain controls and verified logs/IP guidance; a spoofed user-agent GET is a diagnostic, not a verified bot visit. Keep private endpoints inaccessible regardless of robots.

Before first completion and after an authorized content deployment, use the shared [first-delivery verifier](../../niche-site-builder/references/first-delivery.md) and A–Z Q/O/P/I/L/M/N checks. Compare the actual package SHA/source/time, complete public URL inventory, body/author/dates and exports. In an isolated preview inspect scheduled inclusion at T−1/T and revocation; on the real host inspect current responses and caching. Record evidence in `sites/<siteId>/GEO_READINESS.md` or the existing CONTENT_READINESS/audit, avoiding another independent PASS checklist. A failed/missing export is an in-scope integration defect; a missing bot/account log remains explicit unverified evidence.

## Measure without inventing visibility

Technical availability is not indexing, citation, recommendation, attributable visit or qualified demand. Use actual property-scoped first-party reports when connected, otherwise retain `not_connected`/`not_measured`; do not turn absent data into zero. [Measurement](geo-measurement.md) defines consumer-search versus API/vendor samples, fixed prompts, dates/markets, citations and denominators. Refresh relevant research per task through the existing private evidence import. Paid calls and recurring monitoring retain their own scoped budget/schedule; no new paid background system is implied by TXT automation.
