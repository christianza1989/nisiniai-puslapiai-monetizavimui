About, pin numbering & licence

MemoryString is our candidate app name. This edition creates previews and hand-threading instructions. Machine control, physical kit quality and ALINEDECO file compatibility have not been validated.

On-screen numbering starts at pin 1 at the top and increases clockwise. Each project retains its frame layout. Match it to your physical kit before threading. Each colour layer starts with a separate tie-on.
