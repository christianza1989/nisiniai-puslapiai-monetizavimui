The [inner-density brief](.impeccable/surfaces/inner-density.md) owns this scoped refinement; the approved homepage, shared header/footer and global defaults retain their values.

Current local style checks and the separate finish review are in [.impeccable/review/site-style-20261012/](.impeccable/review/site-style-20261012/); this documentation does not certify a new public deployment.
