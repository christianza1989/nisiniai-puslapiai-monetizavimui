## Workflow

Apply [ACQUISITION_CORE](../../ACQUISITION_CORE.md) to the real exchange's purpose, site, contact / provider gates and refusal state. Stop obsolete follow-ups after an answer; OOO and silence are not a new permission. Existing scoped authorization can allow sending, but drafting alone or inbox access cannot.

1. Read the authorized thread/notes/proposal and identify siteId, recipient, previous commitments and requested next step.
2. Draft a concise useful follow-up without invented prices, history, urgency or promises. Dates and terms come from actual records.
3. Keep the draft private; access to the central inbox does not authorize sending or an automatic follow-up sequence.

## Handoff
