# Research a niche demand hypothesis

Read [PROJECT_CONTRACT](../PROJECT_CONTRACT.md) before using this skill in the network. Role: **phase1-support**; it contributes the scoped task below and does not silently expand a domain build. Use current site facts, authorization and actual tool capabilities.

## Workflow

For client acquisition channels or an explicit prospecting experiment, use [niche-client-acquisition](../niche-client-acquisition/SKILL.md) and [ACQUISITION_CORE](../../ACQUISITION_CORE.md). Keep buyer intent separate from supplier / referral interest; channel rights and actual costs are part of feasibility.

1. Use the domain brief and current primary/public evidence to identify customer jobs, alternatives, cost drivers and realistic inquiry information.
2. Label apparent demand, geography and seasonality as hypotheses unless measured. No invented market size, volumes, margins, traffic or competitor weakness.
3. Choose a narrow information/inquiry experiment with explicit evidence that would support expansion; do not require a functioning supplier platform first.

## Handoff

The selected hypothesis, evidence/source dates, alternatives, phase-one test and success measures.
