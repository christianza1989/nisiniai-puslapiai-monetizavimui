# Private acquisition plan

Potential channel: invited TikTok/YouTube creators demonstrating connected
desk workflows. Their demo dashboard previews links, content, referrals,
earnings and payouts. It is not an activated affiliate programme.

Buyer signal: real paid software orders attributed under reviewed terms;
support feedback is useful but not revenue. Creator interest, link clicks and
fictional dashboard balances are separate outcomes. No campaign recipients,
outreach permission or budget are recorded in this integration.

Before any pilot: approve product pricing/terms, creator contract, attribution,
consent/privacy, refund reconciliation, payout process and numerical experiment
thresholds. Use shared acquisition/communication policy for a separately
authorized bounded campaign. No SMTP, Facebook, voice, marketing mailing,
external message or paid service is enabled here.
