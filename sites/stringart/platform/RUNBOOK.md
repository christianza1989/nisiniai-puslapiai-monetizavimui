# Manufacturer demonstration — 7–10 minutes

14 October 2026. Start the local server with the README command, use a desktop browser and select Network operator. Reset fixtures once before the meeting. This is an operating demonstration for a proposed partnership; nothing is sent to a manufacturer and no agreement is implied.

## 0:00–1:00 — establish the fixture boundary

Point to the environment bar: ten synthetic EU studios, fictional contacts/addresses, simulated payment/shipping/telemetry and live adapters off. Overview begins with 16 machine assets and exactly 825 packed kits. The country counts match the business plan. Open Studios → Vilnius to show its profile, fictional address, machine locations/serials and stock.

Explain the three reporting lines: fixture partner sales derive from recorded customer orders; €990 is our separate assumed one-month service revenue at €99 per studio; supplier purchases begin at zero and become received purchase value after reconciliation. These are independent measures, not our turnover or a real distributor network.

## 1:00–4:00 — one portrait from review to delivery

Open Orders → New order. Choose Vilnius, Portrait, a synthetic buyer and Lena attribution. Create it and keep the generated order ID visible.

1. Record the photo suitability review. The supplied AI pet photo is a prepared sample, not a real customer's photo.
2. Save a preview revision using the prepared sample, or a supported exported creator JSON. The preview renders the actual stored sequence. Approve the latest revision through the explicitly simulated buyer action.
3. Simulate payment and reserve stock. The profile is 42 cm, 200 clockwise pins and one black layer; physical compatibility remains pending. Reservation records the exact revision/hash, production and packing quantities.
4. Issue materials and start production on Thread station 1. Boards/pins/thread are consumed now. Covers/boxes stay reserved. Machine control is off.
5. Record passed quality, packing, simulated shipment and delivery. Packing consumes only remaining packing stock. The machine is freed and the order history records every step.
6. Show the held commission and its actual 30-day review-end date. Reload the browser: the same server order and revision remain.

Optional exception within the same time: fail quality, choose station 2 for rework and issue its full replacement production BOM with a reason. The prior station is freed, only one machine owns the job, and extra materials are recorded. Cancellation releases unused reservations; issued materials stay consumed with a write-off trail.

## 4:00–5:30 — DIY inventory without double consumption

Open Inventory. Assemble one kit: one board, 200 pins, 600 m thread, one cover/box/insert are consumed atomically and one packed kit is added. The batch and movement ledger show the BOM version.

Create a Kit order, simulate payment, reserve and pack it. The packed SKU reduces by one; thread and board do not reduce a second time. Ship/deliver it. For a return, inspect it as an unopened kit and restock once; enter a cumulative goods/delivery refund. A damaged kit can be returned without restocking. No activation entitlement or payout is live.

## 5:30–7:00 — partner isolation and supply

Switch to Vilnius partner studio. It sees its own studio/orders/machines/materials and participant transfers, not Berlin's private customer records. Return to the operator and reserve a five-kit transfer from Vilnius to Berlin. Dispatch it: source stock reduces; Berlin only has incoming. Receive it: Berlin gains stock once.

Switch to Equipment & materials supplier → Purchase orders. It sees assigned orders and agreed aggregate SKU demand, with no individual studio/customer photo/project/revenue records. Set an expected receipt date and acknowledge the seeded PO-1001, then dispatch it through the simulated adapter. Switch back to operator → Supply → Reconcile & receive. Only receipt adds the 12,000 m thread and 24 boards to Vilnius stock. The received supplier purchase measure increases by the fixture's €288 assumed value.

## 7:00–8:30 — commission and traceability

Open Referrals. Newly delivered portrait commission remains held. Historical synthetic order MS-LT-100 was delivered on 5 September; its review ended on 5 October. Simulate its eligible payout without changing the clock.

Open that historical order and refund its goods. Return to Referrals: the same commission row now records recovery after the simulated payout. A delivery-only refund leaves goods commission unchanged. Show Activity and the linked stock movements; actor, timestamp, transaction and order IDs explain each change.

## 8:30–10:00 — questions and limits

Show a useful exception if time permits: attempt 100-kit assembly with insufficient components; the error explains shortage, the entered quantity remains, and no stock changes. The unassigned supplier has a proper empty workspace. A stale edit offers refresh instead of overwriting a newer record.

Close with the next commercial gates: authenticate the exact manufacturer/SKU, conformity/software rights, physically test frame and machine output, measure production/material/quality costs and run a real pilot. Candidate brand clearance, territory/exclusivity and actual demand remain unresolved. Retail websites, activation, production auth, live payment/payout/email/shipping and deployment are later phases. The Android creator is preserved as its separately tested local edition.

After the meeting, keep the ledger for review or reset this module's synthetic fixtures. No supplier/customer messages or external actions are part of this runbook.
