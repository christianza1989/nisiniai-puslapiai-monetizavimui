# Evidence, scoring and handover

Each audit records siteId, canonicalHost, phase=1, evaluatedAt, package fingerprint, source/build version, environment and evaluator. Each criterion has ID, status, evidence (paths/URLs plus the observed result), notes and findings where relevant. Checkboxes are marked only after inspecting that evidence. PASS without evidence is rejected. NA requires a reason tied to the real product; no Person profile can be NA when a real Organization is visibly attributed, but missing production access cannot be NA.

Stages are independent: local preparation; real launch; post-launch operations. For each, score = 10 × PASS/applicable criteria, counting FAIL and UNVERIFIED as zero. All criteria carry equal weight; gate items additionally determine readiness. Do not round 9.96 to a displayed 10: report at most two decimals without promoting a non-perfect result. A category score is the same ratio for applicable checks of that letter. Overall/local score never cancels a launch gate.

10/10 means every applicable check of the explicitly named stage has evidence-backed PASS, no gate FAIL/UNVERIFIED, and explicit NA justifications. A missing category, missing check, blank status or missing evidence cannot improve a score. Numeric scores summarize this acceptance rubric, not independent proof of world-class design, legal compliance or immunity to future bugs. Include a separate qualitative visual verdict and a separate live-demand state.

Write per-site `PHASE-1-AUDIT.md` with baseline, P0/P1/P2 findings, repairs and acceptance results, A–Z checkbox rows, stage/category score table and unresolved real launch facts. `PHASE-1-AUDIT.json` contains the same criterion IDs/status/evidence. Use the scorer to detect omissions. A completed audit may honestly conclude NOT READY; completing an audit is different from achieving all acceptance gates.

For a newly completed build use the [first-delivery checkpoint](../../niche-site-builder/references/first-delivery.md) and retain `SITE_COMPLETION.json` from the shared rendered verifier. Bind the current audit via `version.packageSha256`, `version.sourceVersion` and `evaluatedAt`; historic audits remain intact. The scorer's reporting mode intentionally accepts an incomplete audit as a report. Its `--require-local` mode exits nonzero unless every applicable local item, including review criteria, passes. The rendered verifier also exits nonzero for implementation defects or a mismatched/incomplete local audit. Its explicit render-only result is not a full local/launch/platform acceptance.

Repair current local gaps autonomously. Do not postpone a concrete in-scope fix to manufacture a roadmap. If a real business fact, external access, DNS or production control cannot be inferred, finish independent work and record the specific remaining gate; do not invent it. Do not blanket-NA optional readiness requirements merely to reach 10. Report what is functioning and what evidence is still needed.

Initial public deployment must record actual first publication dates. `publishAt` in a not-yet-deployed package is a schedule, even if in the past. A later content review does not rewrite its original publication date; dateModified/lastmod describe a meaningful reviewed change. Source retrieval dates are not publication dates. An organization profile explains actual responsibility and process; author portraits/experience are optional genuine facts.
